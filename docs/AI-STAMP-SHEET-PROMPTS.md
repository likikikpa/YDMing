# Stamp sheet prompts, stand-alone

Every block is complete: rules, style and item list are inside it. Paste one block into a fresh chat of an image generator, wait for the sheet, then cut it out with the prompt at the end. Order follows the roadmap in `AI-STAMP-PROMPTS.md`. File names inside the block are the names to save each stamp under.

Sheet ids: the letter says the style (F fantasy, I ink, R realistic, A atlas), the number the pack.

## Fantasy forests and clumps (F2)

### Sheet F2-1: Temperate forest clumps

```text
Create ONE image, 1536x1024 or larger: a sheet of 6 separate objects in 3 columns x 2 rows, each centred in its own equal cell with at least 10% empty margin on every side, nothing touching or overlapping. Background: one flat pure magenta (#FF00FF), no gradient, no texture, no ground, no shadow on it, no frame; magenta must not appear inside any object. No text, numbers, labels or watermark anywhere, not even on signs or banners. Each object is one complete, isolated, uncropped stamp with a hard clean edge: no glow, haze, semi-transparency or long cast shadow. Light from the top-left on every object. All six objects share the same scale unless noted. Each must still be identifiable when shrunk to 40 px wide: strong silhouette, simplified detail, no hairline strokes. Draw exactly the six objects listed, in reading order (left to right, top to bottom). After the image, list the six file names in that order.

STYLE (fantasy painted map symbols): hand-painted digital illustration, friendly saturated-but-natural colour, a clean dark-brown outline (about 3% of the object's width, slightly thicker on the shaded side, never pure black), soft three-tone cel shading with a faint painterly texture and small bright highlights, shaded side bottom-right. Overland objects use a three-quarter map view (seen from slightly above, front faces visible, like an illustrated fantasy map), not flat top-down and not isometric. Objects marked top-down are seen straight from above with the same outline and shading. Palette: leaf green #4F7F3A to #9DBE4A, warm stone grey #8E8A80, earth brown #8A5B35, terracotta roofs #B5523B, slate roofs, snow #F2F5F7 with blue-grey shade, small accents of gold and red banners. Chunky, readable, slightly exaggerated shapes; group small details into masses.

SHEET F2-1: Temperate forest clumps. A clump is several trees drawn together as one object with an irregular organic outline and no ground under it. Small = 3 trees, medium = 7, dense = 15 packed into a rounded blob.
1. oak-small: small clump of 3 oak trees
2. oak-medium: clump of 7 oak trees
3. oak-dense: dense clump of 15 oak trees
4. mixed-medium: clump of 7 mixed broadleaf trees
5. birch-medium: clump of 7 white-trunked birch trees
6. orchard: rows of fruit trees with a few red and yellow fruits
```
