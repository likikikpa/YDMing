STYLE "Hand-inked, flat": a hand-drawn PEN-AND-INK illustration with FLAT watercolour/gouache colour fills, as printed in an illustrated atlas. Think confident drawing, not rendering. Direction, in order of importance:
1. LARGE SHAPES FIRST. Strong silhouette, simple proportions, big colour masses. Detail is added only where it explains the subject (a door, windows, a roof edge, a tree's trunk and a few clustered leaf masses). Everything else stays QUIET: walls, roof planes, foliage masses, rock faces and snow are mostly flat fills with a calm value change, not covered in texture.
2. LINE. One consistent dark sepia-brown ink outline, about 2-3% of the object's width, slightly irregular like a real pen, never pure black, no hairlines. A few deliberate hatching strokes ONLY on the shaded side to describe form. No outline on every tiny detail.
3. SHADING. Two or three flat tones per material (light, mid, shade) as hard-edged shapes; shade falls bottom-right, light from top-left. NO gradients, NO glow, NO rim light, NO bloom, NO haze, NO sparkles, NO smoke or particle effects (a fire basket is just a flat orange shape; a chimney has no smoke). Shadows stay dark and simple.
4. MATERIALS LOOK DIFFERENT through their drawing, not through noise: thatch = a few long directional strokes; stone = a sparse pattern of block outlines; tile = simple rows of scallops; timber = straight dark beams with a few grain lines; foliage = clustered leaf masses with simple scalloped edges; rock = angular planes with a few hatch strokes; snow = flat white shapes with a pale blue shade.
5. NO universal grain, speckle, scratches, paper noise, distressing or microdetail anywhere. Surfaces are clean. The only texture is deliberate pen marks.
6. PALETTE: muted and limited, earthy: olive and forest greens, warm stone greys, ochre, umber, terracotta, cool blue-grey shade, cream. No neon, no saturated glow colours.
7. Not cinematic, not ornate, not dramatic: simple, friendly, readable, slightly exaggerated shapes. Check it at 48 px wide: the silhouette and colour read clearly, and at full size it looks like deliberate ink-and-wash drawing, not an over-processed render.
