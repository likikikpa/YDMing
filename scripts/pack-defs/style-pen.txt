STYLE "Pen and ink, black and white": a hand-drawn PEN-AND-INK illustration for a map that will be PRINTED ON A BLACK-AND-WHITE PRINTER. Direction, in order of importance:
1. STRICTLY BLACK AND WHITE. Pure black ink lines on pure white. NO colour at all, NO grey wash, NO gradients, NO tinted fills, NO sepia or brown. Every object's interior is filled with solid opaque WHITE (so it hides the map beneath it); tone is made only with black line hatching, cross-hatching and a few stipple dots.
2. LARGE SHAPES FIRST. Strong silhouette, simple proportions. Detail only where it explains the subject (a door, windows, a roof edge, a trunk). Large areas stay white and quiet.
3. LINE. A confident black outline about 2-3% of the object's width, slightly irregular like a real pen, never thinner than 1.5% of the width (no hairlines: it must survive printing small). Hatching strokes are short, parallel, evenly spaced, ONLY on the shaded (bottom-right) side and under overhangs. Light from the top-left.
4. TONE AND DARK MASSES. Solid black and dense hatching are allowed and wanted where the subject is dark: the shaded undersides of boughs and leaf clusters, the gaps inside foliage, deep shade under roofs and eaves, windows, doors. TREES AND BUSHES MUST NOT BE LEFT WHITE (white foliage looks like snow): every bough or leaf cluster is filled with dense parallel hatching or cross-hatching, with only a small white highlight on its top-left edge, so a tree reads as a MID-TO-DARK tone against white ground. Snow is the only thing left white, and only on snow-covered subjects. Overall each object should show a clear range from white to solid black.
5. MATERIALS through the mark, not noise: thatch = long directional strokes; stone = a sparse pattern of block outlines; tile = rows of scallops; timber = straight beams drawn as double lines; foliage = clustered scalloped masses outlined, with a few hatch strokes on the shaded side; rock = angular planes with hatching; snow = left white with outline and shade hatching; water and sky are not drawn.
6. NO grain, speckle, scratches, glow, rim light, smoke, particles or any effect. Clean surfaces. Not cinematic, not ornate: simple, friendly, readable, slightly exaggerated shapes.
7. Check it at 48 px wide in black and white: silhouette and the contrast between dark hatched and white areas must read clearly.
